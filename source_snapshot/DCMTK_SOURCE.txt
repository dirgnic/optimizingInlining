How this attempt uses DCMTK:
- It copies a small standalone subset of DCMTK configuration test sources.
- It compiles them to LLVM IR with Clang without inlining optimizations.
- It extracts callsites and trains/evaluates offline imitation-learning policies
  over the resulting LLVM IR features.
